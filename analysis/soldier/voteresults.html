Methodology

How Weighting Works

Everyone receives 70% of a vote automatically. Up to 30% additional weight is awarded based on lifetime total player hours. Soldier hours are shown as context, but the weighting uses total hours. This page is for transparency and does not hide raw vote totals.
base_weight = 0.70; experience_score = total_hours / max_total_hours; experience_bonus = experience_score * 0.30; final_weight = clamp(base_weight + experience_bonus, 0.70, 1.00)
