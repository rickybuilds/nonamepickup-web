The selected games are replayed oldest to newest using their real recorded team pools. The simulator changes only how each pool is divided among that game's four players; it does not recalculate later match odds. No Name MVP performance is not yet adjusted for role or class.

METHODOLOGY

Fixed-pool replay

Shadow Elo preserves each team’s historical V1 Elo total and only changes how that total is divided among teammates. Match outcomes and historical records are never changed.
