How Predictions Work

A private Discord prediction game with bot points. Completely separate from Elo.

StandingsHow it works

Make your pick in Discord

Choose original Blue / Team 1, original Red / Team 2, or Tie on the match card. Halftime color changes never change these identities. You can replace or cancel one active pick before betting closes; stakes are reserved immediately.

The official eight players, offense participants and former participants in the match cannot bet. Only the official eight receive player rewards.

A full two-minute closing window

When the bot confirms all eight official players on the assigned game server, it starts a full 120-second window. Gameplay does not shorten it. A restart command schedules roster checks; it never starts or resets the countdown. Deadlines and game status come from the bot.

Points and personal limits

The bot grants 1,000 points once, on the first eligible private account interaction or player reward. Reading this website never initializes an account or grants points.

New games start with a personal maximum of 100 points, increasing by 10 per distinct settled game you bet on. Wins, losses, ties and legacy settled no-correct-bettor refunds count. Edits, cancellations, voids, pregame refunds and player rewards do not. Corrections never count a game twice, and later review does not remove earned progression.

Legacy rule-version-1 games retain a fixed 250-point maximum, including replacement revisions. All stakes must fit the available balance and safe-integer representation; there is no fixed business cap on progression.

How the pot is shared

Correct stakes return first. The remaining pot is split as follows:

  • Win/loss: 70% to correct bettors, 25% to winning players, 5% to losing players.
  • Tie: 90% to correct bettors, 10% to the official eight.
  • No correct bettors in new rule-version-3 games: the house keeps all stakes. Losing bettors receive no refund, and players receive no rewards. Earlier games keep their original refund rule.

Bettor shares are proportional to correct stakes; player shares are equal. Stable integer rounding conserves the full pot.

Corrections and standings

Owner corrections preserve all previous credits and pay only positive differences, funded by the house. No clawbacks, negative balances or repeat correction credits. Actual retained profit and corrected pick accuracy can therefore differ.

Standings are ranked by finalized net betting profit. It excludes grants, owner adjustments, player-only rewards and pregame refunds. Reviews stay separate. Equal profit shares a rank; the next rank skips tied places. All existing prediction accounts are included, with no minimum games. Inactive players remain in the standings.
