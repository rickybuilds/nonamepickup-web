Ringers and player addition

Ringers may be used if both teams are in agreement. A clan / team may only have 1 ringer. If a clan has less than 4 players (including ringers) the team forfeits the match.

New players to the league can be added to clans after discussion with Ricky and captains.

CTF: By default it is 5O VS 5D, however if both teams decide, it can be 3D VS 2O. It will remain 2 matches in either format.

Time limits

  • ADL: Default (no pre-match, use live on restart)
  • CTF: 20 minutes

For ADL, two rounds will be played. The first team will be on offense round 1 and the second team will be offense on round 2.

There should be a small break in between matches (5–10 minutes) to allow player switchover.

If start time or the break time exceeds 20 minutes, a forfeit can be requested.

Tie breakers

Team that was on D will go O on restart. After they cap the round will restart with the alternate team being O / D.

The team that captures the flag (CTF) or the first capture point (ADL) quickest wins (in seconds).

If both teams do not have time to play this out, logs will be used to determine by second who capped quickest on the final cap.

Match times

The standard times will be 8:00 PM Eastern on Sunday and Saturday 3:00 PM Eastern (this time will rotate). There are currently 3 servers that will be used for this: PIP, OST, and IODINE.

This time can be rescheduled to anytime Friday–Thursday, it just has to be agreed upon by all teams.

Cheating and abusing

  • Speed hack: Removal from league.
  • Suspected auto-aim / wall hacker: 1st violation: banned for 3 weeks / 2nd violation: removal from league.
  • Chop Hop: 1st violation: warning / 2nd violation: banned for 1 week / 3rd violation: banned for 3 weeks / 4th violation: removal from league.

1 duck is allowed for doing ramp slides.

In CTF, unless the enemy has the flag / you are defending flag, you cannot attack offense beyond the mid way mark of the map. Repeated violations will result in short term player bans.

If a player cheats or if a chop hop violation results in a cap, the other team automatically wins.

Respect, racism, and orderly conduct

All players are asked to show respect to each other. We want to avoid constant cussing, all racism, and purposely wrecking the quality of the game by trolling and treating others poorly. These matches will be streamed, so we want to keep things clean.

The following issues will be voted on by the community:

  • Blatant racism: 1st violation: removal from match and banned for 1 additional week / 2nd violation: banned for 5 weeks / 3rd violation: removal from league.
  • Trolling / constantly disrespecting: 1st violation: banned for 1 week / 2nd violation: banned for 3 weeks / 3rd violation: removal from league.
