How to read these ratings

Higher is better: positive offense means more team captures; positive defense means fewer conceded. These are team-result associations, not individual contributions. Teammates, opponents, roles, and small samples affect every rating.

What is Defense Rating?

Map baseline captures conceded minus the player’s team captures conceded. Higher is better. This measures team results associated with a player’s defensive appearances.

Estimated appearances require linked defensive role and class records, at least 70% dominant class time and adequate coverage.
